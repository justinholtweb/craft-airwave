On the page

One line,
or none at all

A tag in a template, a reference tag in rich text, or a Feed field an author fills in themselves.

  • Layouts: list, cards or titles
  • Layout-only CSS — a feed looks like the page it is on
  • Or override the markup entirely and loop over the items
In a template
{{ craft.airwave.render('craft-news') }}
In any rich-text field
{airwave:craft-news:render}
— no template change, no editor plugin
Several feeds, one stream (Pro)
{{ craft.airwave.renderMerged(['craft-news', 'php-weekly']) }}

Filters  ·  Pro

Filtered on
the way in

An item that was never wanted is never stored — and therefore can never turn up in an entry by accident.

  • Keywords, categories, authors, maximum age
  • Undated items are kept, not silently dropped

Channels

Publish
your own

A channel is a URI on your site plus enough configuration to answer it. Point it at a section — or at the feeds Airwave has already collected.

  • Cached, with a matching Cache-Control
  • Aggregated markup is purified again on the way out
RSS/blog.rssSection: Blog
Atom/news.atomSection: News
JSON/feed.jsonSection: Blog
RSS/best-of-the-web.xmlCollected items

That last one is a curated feed of everything you subscribe to, published under your domain, without a template.

Console

Every feed,
and its health

Refresh on a schedule, find the feeds a site advertises, or parse a URL without subscribing to it.

  • A failed refresh never empties a feed
  • --due respects each feed’s own interval
$ php craft airwave/feeds
craft-news  ·  48 items  ·  healthy  ·  12m ago
php-weekly  ·  30 items  ·  stale    ·  6h ago
$ php craft airwave/feeds/refresh --due
2 feeds refreshed, 14 new items, 3 filtered out.
$ php craft airwave/feeds/discover example.com
Found 2  ·  /feed.xml (RSS)  ·  /feed.json (JSON Feed)
